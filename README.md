# Valenscope

Model your net worth over time. Track assets, debts, income, and expenses. Plan for retirement, financial independence, and other money milestones. Includes personalized financial insights powered by AI.

## Features

- **Net worth tracking** — add assets and debts to see your current net worth at a glance.
- **Cash flow modeling** — log income and expenses to project how your balance sheet changes over time.
- **Milestone planning** — set goals like retirement or financial independence and see when you'll hit them based on your current trajectory.
- **AI insights** — get personalized financial observations powered by Gemini AI based on your data.
- **Charts** — visualize your net worth projection over time with Chart.js.
